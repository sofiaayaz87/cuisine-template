Admin Panel
Manage restaurant data, user accounts, and feedback.
Approve or reject new restaurant submissions.
Generate and view analytical reports.
User Management	
OTP-based registration and login using email verification.
Manage user profiles, history, and feedback records.
Restaurant Information Management
Add, update, delete, and view restaurant records (name, cuisine, price range, location, contact).
Menu Management
Maintain menus for each restaurant including dishes, categories, and prices.
Recommendation System
Suggest restaurants based on location, mood, cuisine preference, and previous activity using a hybrid model (Content-Based + Collaborative Filtering).
Map Visualization
Display restaurant locations on interactive maps using stored latitude and longitude values.
Chatbot Assistant
A rule-based chatbot that helps users find restaurants by handling keyword-based queries.
Favorites and History Tracking
Users can bookmark restaurants and view their search history.
Data used to refine recommendation results.
Feedback and Reviews
Users can rate and review restaurants.
Reviews are stored for analysis and quality improvement.
Inventory Management
Track ingredients, stock levels, and supplier details for each restaurant branch.
Notify admins when items are low in stock.
Employee Management
Maintain employee records, assign staff to specific restaurants, and monitor performance.
Reports and Analytics
Admin can generate detailed reports about top-rated restaurants, user trends, and sales data.
Reservation & Table Management
Customers can book, update, or cancel reservations in real time.
Admin can monitor upcoming bookings and table availability.
 Promotion & Discount Management
Admin can create, update, and manage promotional offers or loyalty programs.
Discount data is integrated with billing and order modules.
 Branch & Performance Management
Manage multiple restaurant branches under one system.
Compare performance metrics such as sales, customer ratings, and order frequency.
Security & Access Control
Role-based access for Admin, Manager, Employee, and Customer.
Ensures secure login, restricted access, and proper authorization flow.
